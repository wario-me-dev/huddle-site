Product Update November 17, 2026

Stay in the Loop, Your Way

2 min read The Huddle Team
✦ Summary

New notification types, familiar faces in your chat alerts, and more control over what you hear from and see on Huddle. Choose how often each World notifies you, mute Worlds from your feeds, and explore a new discovery tab.

This update is all about making Huddle work a little better for you. We're bringing you more ways to stay in the loop, along with the settings to decide how much you want to hear.

See Who's Messaging You

Chat notifications now show profile or Capsule pictures alongside the Huddle logo, making it easier to tell which chat a message is from.

Whether it's a friend checking in or your Capsule coming alive, you'll see a familiar picture before you even open Huddle.

New Notifications for Worlds

We've added new notifications for Worlds, including likes, to help you keep up with what's happening in your communities. And you get to choose how often you hear from each one.

Off, Low, or Frequent

You can now set each World's alerts to Off, Low, or Frequent.

Off

No push notifications

Low

Trending posts only

Frequent

Every post

Want frequent updates from your favorite fandom? Prefer to hear less from another? You can set them individually to a level that works for you.

Mute Worlds From Your Feeds

You can now mute Worlds to keep them off your feeds completely.

If a World isn't your thing or your interests have changed, you can make more room for what you want to see.

Also New: Worlds Discovery

Our new Worlds discovery tab helps you find more communities based on your interests and the topics you love. Take a look and see where your next obsession takes you.

Fixes and Improvements

We've also fixed a few things that were getting in the way:

Fixed attachments failing to upload on slow connections or when uploads timed out.
AndroidFixed the notification pop-up on Home appearing every time.
AndroidTurning on notifications from the Home pop-up now also enables them in your profile.
AndroidFixed a crash on launch affecting some devices.

Update Huddle and make yourself at home. There's more on the way, and we're excited to share it with you.

Keep huddling!

- The Huddle Team

Product Update October 21, 2026

Introducing Worlds: Where Fandoms Live

3 min read Viaano Spruyt
✦ Summary

Worlds is officially live, and it's the biggest update we've ever shipped. Every World is a dedicated community built around a fandom you love, with a shared feed for fan art, photos, and discussions, plus events run by World owners. This release also brings up to 9 attachments per post, personalized onboarding, and a long list of fixes.

Worlds is finally here, and it's the biggest update we've ever shipped on Huddle.

We're bringing fandoms together in a whole new way, giving fans a place to find their people, share what they love, and be part of communities that feel like home.

What Are Worlds?

Worlds are fandoms brought to life.

Each World is a dedicated community built around something you love, whether that's your favorite anime, K-pop group, game, or anything in between.

Share

Fan art, photos, questions, and discussions, all in one shared public feed.

Gather

World owners can organize events and bring their community together.

Grow

Owners get the tools to shape their World and help their community grow.

Whether you're keeping up with your favorite fandom or discovering something completely new, Worlds makes it easier than ever to connect with people who share your interests.

Why We're Building Worlds

Our goal has always been to bring people together through the things they love.

We want Huddle to be more than just another social app. We're building a digital third space for fans: somewhere you can explore your interests, express yourself freely, discover new communities, and connect with people who understand your passions.

We believe fandoms are a big part of how people express themselves, discover who they are, and form meaningful connections. That's why we're building Huddle around self-expression, with digital avatars, highly customizable profiles, and features that let your personality and interests shine through.

Our vision is to build a next-generation social network where discovering new communities, conversations, and people feels natural and exciting.

Worlds is our biggest step toward making that vision a reality.

What Else Is New?

Alongside Worlds, we've introduced several improvements across Huddle to make your experience even better.

Up to 9 attachments 3 → 9

Share more of what you love by adding up to 9 attachments to a single post, up from 3.

Customizable World About tabs

World owners can now personalize their Worlds with bios, rules, links, and custom sections to make their communities more welcoming.

Better photo viewing

You can now tap photos directly from your feed to open and view them.

Improved navigation

The bottom navigation bar now stays visible inside Worlds, making it easier to move around Huddle.

Quick profile access

Your profile picture now appears in the bottom navigation bar, so your profile is always just one tap away.

Personalized onboarding

Choose your interests when joining Huddle to help you discover communities that match what you love.

Simpler signup

Profile pictures and cover photos are no longer required when creating an account.

Fixes and Improvements

We've also fixed several issues to make Huddle smoother, faster, and more reliable.

Fixed random logouts
Fixed verification code errors and other signup issues
Fixed a bug that caused the app to freeze
Fixed issues with chat notifications
The back button now returns you to the same tab and scroll position
Improved HOT sorting in Worlds so new posts appear immediately, while posts older than 14 days remain visible

We'd Love to Hear From You

200+

World owners helped us beta test, refine, and improve Worlds before its official release.

Worlds wouldn't be possible without our community, and I'm incredibly grateful to everyone who helped bring this to life.

Your experience on Huddle matters deeply to me. If you have any suggestions, ideas, or feedback, please share them in the ~suggestions World on Huddle or open a ticket in our official Discord server.

This is a huge milestone for Huddle, and there's still so much more we want to build.

Thank you for being part of Huddle and for coming along on this journey with us. It means more than you know.

Viaano Spruyt

Co-Founder, Huddle
